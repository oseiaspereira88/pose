POSE can aggregate everything still owed into one read-only answer: unmet prerequisites and unexplained blocks from readiness, closeout and review obligations of in-progress specs (including each judgment criterion a sealed bundle still owes), start reconciliation when atomic start is adopted, and open follow-ups as advisory residual debt. Every answer is bound to a snapshot (revision, dirty working tree, policy and contract digests, delivery index) and lists per-producer coverage; a producer that fails or is not integrated yet is shown as such, never as zero obligations, and a later change of policy or revision is detectable before any write.
