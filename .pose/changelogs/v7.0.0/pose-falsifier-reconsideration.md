A material decision can name its `Expected effect:` and a `Falsifier check: check:<module>/<check>`. When the delivery index holds a failed result for that check, the obligation projection emits a `falsifier-observed` reconsideration candidate on the decision, advisory and addressed to a person. The decision and its rationale are never edited, no cause is inferred, and small decisions need neither field.
