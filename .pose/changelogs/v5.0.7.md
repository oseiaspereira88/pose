# POSE v5.0.7

## Changed

- The CLI prints through one rendering layer. The result — a verdict, its findings, the data you asked for — goes to stdout, and progress, usage and the command's own failures go to stderr, so `pose check --json | jq` is safe and `2>/dev/null` no longer hides a finding. A finding carries its remediation, colour and symbols appear only on a terminal (with an ASCII profile where UTF-8 cannot be trusted, and never as the only carrier of meaning), and `--color`, `NO_COLOR`, `POSE_COLOR`, `POSE_LOCALE` and `COLUMNS` decide what a stream gets, resolved per stream.  `pose validate` now reports each check as a step with its outcome and duration — a live status line on a terminal, one line per event anywhere else — and captures a check's output instead of streaming it, showing a failing one's tail with a pointer to the full result; `--verbose` restores streaming. `pose check` gains `--json`, `--quiet` and `--color`, and `--json` prints one document carrying the verdict, the findings and the counts.  **Breaking.** Findings that used to reach stderr now reach stdout (`lint-spec`, `knowledge-usage`), `pose validate`'s progress moved to stderr and it no longer streams a passing check's output, and `pose check`'s human line no longer carries the `[ERRO]`/`[AVISO]` anchor — that anchor is the untranslated `severity` field of `check --json`. Scripts that redirect a stream or grep those lines need updating; `pose report` no longer parses printed output at all. (pose-cli-output-rendering-system)
