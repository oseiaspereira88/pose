A new instance now starts with a governed first spec, `.pose/specs/<date>-pose-onboarding.md`, in the install's locale: adopting POSE is the project's first piece of work — declared identity, a maintainer who can prove answers, the commit gate, reviewed capability defaults and the committed instance — with tasks that point to `pose setup`. It passes lint, the strict check and the Definition of Ready as written; `pose setup` names `pose start spec:pose-onboarding` first and its close once every other step is done. `pose update` and reinstalls never create or overwrite it.
