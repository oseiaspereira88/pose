`pose followups --candidates` lists open follow-ups worth reconciling — a named spec that is now done, a named test that now exists, a passed review date, lexical near-duplicates — each with its evidence and the limits of that evidence. Nothing is closed or marked duplicate automatically; Attention shows the same reason on the follow-up, and the disposition stays a written decision in the spec.
