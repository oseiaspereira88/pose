## Scope

New lifecycle events distinguish recipient-authorized waiver, requester withdrawal
and authorized predecessor retirement. Operation-bound SSHSIG uses
`pose-action-lifecycle`; schema-2 issuer claims bind operation/revision/reason/key
and successor identity. Neither proof can be replaced by a label or an answer
signature. The protected predecessor append reloads authority and proof before
writing. The shared reader reverifies recorded lifecycle proofs rather than
trusting assurance labels. Historical expiry is evaluated at acceptance; current
issuer trust pins remain the existing revocation boundary. Local declared policy
remains declared; MCP actor lifecycle writes always require proof. Arbitrary actor
invalidation is refused; observed subject-change/transfer behavior stays with the
engine. CLI, MCP, English/Portuguese manuals and scaffold describe these effects.

The separate review found F1: transport preview/apply regenerated RequestedAt,
changing the successor ID while external signing occurred. Timestamp preservation
and RFC3339 validation correct it; signed positive controls exercise real CLI/MCP
with an earlier fixed timestamp and verify lineage. The reviewer approved the
corrected code in round 2. See [separate review](2026-10-24-b09-independent-review.md)
for identity, criteria and limits; no cross-vendor conclusion is claimed.
